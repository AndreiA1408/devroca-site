Built for your kind of business.

Six common kinds of project, and the services each one is usually built from. These are examples of what fits, not a portfolio of past clients.

I work with small businesses and large companies, in any industry — and because it’s one person behind every project, you get direct, consistent attention rather than a rotating team or an account manager relaying messages.

Business types we build for

Clinics & Healthcare

Patients find a service, see who provides it, and book without phoning the front desk.

  • Booking built into the site, with automated confirmations
  • Service and practitioner pages, built from your information
  • Accessible, mobile-responsive build
Often built with: Website Development · Online Booking Systems · Google Reviews Integration

Restaurants & Hospitality

Menu, opening hours, and reservations on a site that loads fast on a phone.

  • Menu as real page content, not a PDF
  • Reservation flow with automated confirmations
  • Extra languages, with translations supplied by you
Often built with: Website Development · Online Booking Systems · Multi-Language Support · Content Update Subscription

Retail & E-commerce

A product catalog, and a checkout that handles failed payments, refunds, and receipts — not just the happy path.

  • Catalog with categories and filtering
  • Checkout wired to Stripe, PayPal, or a local processor
  • Admin dashboard to manage orders and stock levels
Often built with: Web App Development · Payment Gateway Integration · Analytics & Event Tracking Setup

Service & Booking Platforms

Scheduling-first web apps for businesses whose whole model runs on appointments — where booking is the product, not one feature of a site.

  • Calendar & availability logic
  • Automated confirmations
  • Dashboard for managing bookings
Often built with: Web App Development · Payment Gateway Integration · Maintenance & Support

Professional Services & Consultancies

A fast, clean site for your services and case studies, built from the material you already have.

  • Service and case-study pages
  • Enquiry form, plus newsletter signup wired to your email platform
  • Technical SEO so pages are indexed properly
Often built with: Website Development · Email Capture & Automation Integration · Technical SEO Implementation

Fitness & Wellness

Class timetables and booking for a business that runs on a weekly schedule.

  • Class and session booking
  • Timetable and instructor pages
  • Payment taken at booking, per class or session
Often built with: Website Development · Online Booking Systems · Payment Gateway Integration

Don't see your kind of business?

The list above covers the common cases, not every case. Tell me what you’re working with and I’ll tell you honestly whether it’s a fit.

HOW IT WORKS

From conversation to custom quote.

Four steps from the ranges above to a number that doesn't move once it's agreed.

You describe it

What the business does, what the site or app has to do, and any deadline you're working to.

We scope it

A short conversation to turn that into a specific list of services — the pages, the functionality, the integrations.

You get a custom quote

Each service priced within its published range, totalled into one number, in writing, with the list of what it covers. No hourly estimate that drifts.

Work starts

The price holds for the agreed scope. If you later want something added, it's quoted separately before it's built.

WHAT MOVES THE NUMBER

What the price is based on.

The ranges above are wide because projects are. Three things decide where inside a range your own number lands.

Which services are included

A five-page site is a different job from a web app with logins, a booking system, and a second language. The total is assembled from the services you actually pick, each within its own range.

  • Priced per service, not per bundle
  • Nothing included that you didn't ask for

How much of each

Page count, number of languages, how much custom functionality sits behind the front end — this is what moves a service from the bottom of its range to the top.

  • Scope stated in numbers, not adjectives
  • Written down before the price is quoted

One-off vs. ongoing

Build work is a one-time price quoted for the agreed scope. Maintenance, monitoring, and content updates are separate monthly amounts, quoted the same way.

  • Build and retainer priced separately
  • Monthly items billed monthly, no lock-in

What the quote itself contains

The quote is a written document, not a number in a chat message. Every one has the same four parts, so you can read it before agreeing to anything.

  1. The service line itemsWhich of the 15 services are in scope, named individually.
  2. The numbers behind themPage count, number of languages, which functionality — scope stated in figures, not adjectives.
  3. The quoted priceOne total for that scope, each line within its published range, plus any monthly amounts listed separately.
  4. What isn't includedAnything discussed but left out, written down, so a later addition is an obvious separate quote.

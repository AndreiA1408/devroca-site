How a project actually goes.

No black box. Here's exactly what happens from the first conversation to an ongoing relationship.

Project stages

01

Discovery

We talk through your business, your goals, and what "done" actually looks like for you — before anything gets designed or built.

  • A short call or written brief about your business and audience
  • Clarifying which services actually fit
  • A clear, specific quote — not a vague estimate
You end up with: a written scope — the specific services in your project — and one price quoted for that scope, set to be competitive for what it covers.
TYPICALLY 1–3 DAYS
02

Design & Build

Your site or app takes shape, with regular check-ins so there are no surprises when it's time to launch.

  • Structure and design direction shared early for feedback
  • Development in visible stages, not a black box until the end
  • Room for revisions as it comes together
You end up with: a working site or app you've already seen at each stage, with your feedback built in.
TYPICALLY 1–4 WEEKS, DEPENDING ON SCOPE
03

Launch

We ship it, test it thoroughly, and make sure everything works exactly as expected before it goes live.

  • Cross-device and cross-browser testing
  • Domain and hosting connected
  • A final walkthrough so you know how everything works
You end up with: the site live on your own domain, tested across devices, plus a walkthrough of how to run it.
TYPICALLY 1–2 DAYS
04

Grow

Ongoing maintenance, plus any add-ons you want, whenever you're ready for them — the relationship doesn't end at launch.

  • Monthly maintenance and monitoring if you want it
  • Add booking, multi-language, integrations, or anything else later
  • No pressure, no long-term contract required
You end up with: a maintained site and an open door — add-ons get quoted the same way, whenever you want them.
